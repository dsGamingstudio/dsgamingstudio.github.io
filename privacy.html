Privacy Policy for Snug Street

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Snug Street ("the game") is made by dsGaming, the trading name of Parris Warner ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to dsgames.gamedev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game has no accounts.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to find crashes, and (once ads are added) AppLovin and its ad partners to show ads. These services receive device information and an advertising identifier.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, no analytics, crash reports or ads start until you answer the consent screen, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. The one exception is a small settings download that keeps the game working (see "Game settings" below).
  • In the United States you can turn on "Don't sell or share my info" in Settings.
  • To have your data deleted, email us the Player ID shown in Settings.

1. Information the game collects

Stored only on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, shop restoration, settings, purchases owned) is stored in a file on your device. It also holds a random Player ID created when the game is first opened. The Player ID does not identify you by name.

Game settings (for everyone, before the consent screen)

Each time it starts, the game downloads its current settings (such as level difficulty, prices and maintenance notices) from Google Firebase Remote Config. To answer, Google receives your IP address, the Firebase installation ID and basic app information (app version, device language). This is needed for the game to work and is not used for analytics or ads, so it happens before the consent screen too; it relies on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working.

Collected by the services the game uses (only with consent where the law requires it)

  • Game usage: events such as a level started, won or lost, boosters used, coins earned and spent, tutorial steps, settings changed, and how long a level took.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, country or region (derived from your IP address; not precise location), app version, and time zone.
  • Identifiers: the Player ID, the Firebase installation ID, and your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), which you can reset or delete in your device settings.
  • Crash reports: technical details of a crash (stack traces, device state).
  • Purchases: if you buy something, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a record of the purchase (product, time, order reference), never your card or payment details.
  • Ads (once ads are added): information about the ads shown, interactions with them, and the identifiers above, used to choose and measure ads.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the game, keep your progress, and deliver purchases.
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, fix bugs and crashes, and improve the game.
  • To show ads that pay for the free game, to limit how often you see them, and to measure them.
  • To prevent cheating and fraud, and to meet legal obligations.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we rely on your consent for analytics, crash reporting and ads, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure for the rest. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy choices; this does not affect anything done before.

3. Who receives it

We do not sell your information for money. The game shares information with these service providers, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Play services, Google Play billing, and Google AdMob as an ad partner): policies.google.com/privacy, and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • AppLovin (AppLovin MAX, the service that chooses which ad partner shows an ad): applovin.com/privacy.
  • Ad partners that may show ads through AppLovin: Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network (facebook.com/privacy/policy), Unity Ads (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) and Mintegral (mintegral.com/en/privacy).

Under some US state laws, sharing an advertising ID with ad partners for targeted ads can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out as described in section 5.

These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, using the safeguards the law requires (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

4. How long it is kept

Progress on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics events are kept by Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Ad partners keep data as their own policies describe. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): answer the consent screen when the game first opens, and change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • "Don't sell or share my info" (United States and elsewhere outside Europe): turn it on in Settings. Ads then stop using your information for targeting; you may still see ads that are not personalised.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, portability: email dsgames.gamedev@gmail.com with the Player ID from the game's Settings. We will answer within 30 days (45 where US state law allows) and ask the services above to delete the data linked to that ID. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Complaints: in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you can also complain to your data protection authority.

6. Children

Snug Street is meant for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and we use reputable providers. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what we hold.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the game.
